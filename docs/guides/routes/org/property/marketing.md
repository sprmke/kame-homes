# Marketing — operator guide

> **Telegram marketing** settings live on **[Notifications](./notifications.md)** (`…/notifications?module=marketing`). This guide covers **Marketing Content Studio** (design templates + Meta publishing).

Route: `/org/:orgSlug/property/:propertySlug/marketing`

> **Status:** Documented — Content Studio + Telegram (see Notifications)

Legacy redirect: `/marketing` → `…/property/:propertySlug/marketing` (Content Studio)

## Overview

Property-scoped **Marketing Content Studio**: pick a **theme** (colors, fonts, background, corners), then build availability calendars, social posts and short promo videos in it, or **generate a finished image from a prompt plus your own photos**, then download or publish to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram. Telegram marketing alerts live on **Notifications**, not this page.

Saved marketing designs are bulk-copyable via org **Properties → Copy settings** (publication history is not).

---

## Host-facing knowledge

Marketing Content Studio is where you create promotional content for this property: availability calendars, social posts, short videos, and AI-generated photos. You start by picking a theme, and everything you make in it shares the same colors and fonts. You can download what you make or publish it straight to your connected Facebook and Instagram accounts. Marketing Telegram alerts live in a separate place, under Notifications.

**Common host questions**

- Q: What is a theme?
  A: A theme is your look: a few colors, three fonts, a background style and a corner style. Calendars, posts, videos and AI results you make inside a theme all use it, so your marketing looks like one brand.
- Q: Why did Marketing open on a page of themes?
  A: That is your first visit. Pick a theme to start (Property colors uses your photos or brand color). Next time, Marketing opens straight into the last theme you used. Tap **Themes** at the start of the theme bar (top right on a computer, top of the editor on a phone) to come back to this page.
- Q: Why don't I see all the starter looks?
  A: The themes page only lists your own themes, so it stays short. **New theme** is the first card. Tap it to see the starter looks: one per style first, and **More looks** shows the rest. Each look shows a sample calendar, post and video.
- Q: How do I switch themes?
  A: Tap the theme name in the theme bar and pick another one. Each theme keeps its own calendars, posts and videos.
- Q: Can I change a theme's colors or fonts?
  A: Yes. Tap **Edit theme** (or **Customize** on a built-in theme) next to the theme name. Pick a palette, then tap any of the five colors (Main, Highlight, Soft tone, Background, Text) to make it lighter or darker or pick another one. Choose a font pairing (**Pick each font** sets them one by one), a background and a corner style. The preview is a sample post, calendar and clip in those colors. Your saved work stays in the library.
- Q: I edited my theme. What happens to things I already made?
  A: They update to the new look the next time you open them. Anything you changed by hand (a color you picked in Advanced, a font you changed on a post) stays the way you set it.
- Q: Can AI make a theme for me?
  A: Yes. In **New theme**, type what you want under **Describe a look**, for example "calm beach house, sandy and soft blue", and tap **Generate**. The colors, fonts, background, corners and photo mood fill in so you can adjust them before saving. It uses your AI credits like the other Generate buttons.
- Q: What is **Photo mood** on a theme?
  A: It is under **More options** in the theme editor. It picks the starting light and mood (Golden hour, Bright and airy and so on) for AI photos and videos you make in that theme. You can still change it on each photo.
- Q: Can I edit a starter look like Bubble Pop?
  A: Starter looks stay as they are. Pick it in **New theme** (or tap **Customize** while you work in it) and save: you get your own copy to change, and the new theme opens. On plans without saved themes, picking a look opens it as it is.
- Q: What happens to my items if I delete a theme?
  A: Nothing is deleted. You pick another theme and the items move there, taking on its look.
- Q: Why do the calendar templates look different in each theme?
  A: The calendar templates are layouts. They keep their shape (rounded dots, blocks, ruled grids) and take the colors and fonts of the theme you are in. Pick the starter look with the same name to see a layout in its original colors.
- Q: Where are the calendar style controls?
  A: The Calendar sidebar shows the everyday options: layouts, size, and **Show on calendar** (property name, subtitle, legend and so on). Tap **Advanced** for full control over every part of the calendar.

- Q: Where are the things I made?
  A: Open **Posts**, **Videos** or **Calendar**. Each tab opens on your library: everything you made in this theme, newest first. Tap a card to keep working on it. Turn on **All themes** to see items from your other themes too.
- Q: How do I start something new?
  A: Tap **New post** (or New video, New calendar) and pick a template, or start from **Blank** (and **Collage** for posts). The first time you open a tab, the templates are right there.
- Q: What is a new calendar called?
  A: The theme name plus calendar, such as Desk Buddy calendar. The next one in that theme is Desk Buddy calendar 2. Tap the name to change it. A calendar started from one of your templates keeps that template's name.
- Q: Does opening a template change the template?
  A: No. A template is a starting point. Nothing is saved until you change something, and then it saves as your own new item. The template stays as it was.
- Q: Will my edits save automatically?
  A: Yes, once you change something. The top bar shows **Saving…** then **Saved**. Reopening an item and looking at it does not create a copy or move it to the top of your list.
- Q: I opened the same template again. Why does it ask Continue or Start new?
  A: You already made something from it recently. **Continue** opens that item; **Start new** begins a fresh one from the template.
- Q: How do I make a copy?
  A: In your library, open the **⋯** menu on a card and tap **Duplicate**. Inside the editor, the size menu can also make a copy in another size.
- Q: How do I make my own template?
  A: Open the **⋯** menu on one of your items and tap **Save as template**. It shows under **Your templates** in the template list and on the library. Rename or delete it from that menu. Built-in templates cannot be renamed or deleted.
- Q: Can I make the same post in another size?
  A: Yes. Use the size menu in the editor. **Resize** changes this item, **Copy** makes a new item in the new size that stays grouped with this one. Grouped items show as one card with "N sizes".
- Q: I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?
  A: Yes, for 30 days. Tap **Undo** right after deleting, or open **History**, choose **Recently deleted**, and tap **Restore**. On a phone, that menu is **More**. After 30 days it is gone for good.
- Q: Why does the editor say someone else changed this?
  A: The same item was saved from another tab or by a teammate. Tap **Reload** to see their version, or **Keep mine** to save yours over it.
- Q: Do I need to connect Facebook before I can publish?
  A: Yes. Connect your Facebook Page and Instagram through Guest Inbox first. Without that connection, you can still design and download assets, just not publish from here.
- Q: Can I turn a guest review into a social post?
  A: Yes. In the post or video editor, open **Reviews** in the left rail and pick a Kame or Facebook review (Airbnb quotes stay browse-only). It fills a review layout or a **Guest love** / **Stay again** reel. You can still edit it before you download or publish.
- Q: Can AI make a post, video or calendar for me inside the editor?
  A: Yes. Open **AI** in the editor's left rail, describe what you want and tap Generate. You get one new item in the size you are working on; use the size menu for other sizes.
- Q: What is **AI Studio** for?
  A: It is the first tab, where AI makes things for you. Pick **Post** (a finished design from your photos), **Photo** (a new picture of your place) or **Clip** (a short video). On a wider screen the form sits on the left and a live preview sits on the right, so you can see what you are setting up before you generate. Everything you make is kept as its own card, and you can open it later and keep improving it.
- Q: How do I keep improving something AI made?
  A: Open it from **Your creations**. The preview is on the right, your conversation and every version on the left. Type what to change ("shorter headline", "make it evening") and send. The new version joins the list; nothing you already made is lost, and you can switch back to any version.
- Q: How do I make a post with AI?
  A: Tap **New post**, under **What's it about?** pick Vibe, Promo, Amenities, Feature or Stay info, optionally say more in the text box, pick a size, then **Generate 4 posts**. You get four designs made from your own photos and open on the best-laid-out one.
- Q: Where do the times, deposit and amenities on AI posts come from?
  A: From your property settings, word for word. The AI writes the headline and short lines, but it cannot add an amenity, price or time you have not saved. Update Settings if something is missing.
- Q: Can I change an AI post without using credits?
  A: Yes. **Shuffle layout** (in the ⋯ menu) tries a different layout with the same words, and changing the size redraws it for that shape. Both make a new version and cost nothing. Typing a change instead asks the AI and uses credits.
- Q: How do I use an AI post in the Posts editor?
  A: Tap **Edit in Posts**. It saves as one of your posts and opens in the editor, where you can change anything by hand.
- Q: Why does the Posts editor show a 4:5 Portrait size only for some posts?
  A: Portrait 4:5 comes from AI Studio. It appears in the size menu while one of those posts is open, and you can switch it to the other sizes like any post.
- Q: Why did I get simple layouts instead of AI ones?
  A: If AI styling is busy you get quick layouts built from the same design set, so you still have posts to use. Try again in a moment for AI-picked styles.
- Q: How do I make a photo that looks like my place?
  A: Tap **New photo**, describe the shot, then **Add photos** and pick a few from your listing (or upload your own). The AI builds the new photo from those, so the room, view and finishes look like yours.
- Q: I asked for a change to a photo and it changed everything. Why?
  A: It should not. A change starts from the version on screen and only does what you asked. Keep each request to one thing ("warmer light", "no plant on the table") and send them one at a time.
- Q: What does **Look** do?
  A: It sets the light and mood (Golden hour, Bright and airy, Evening glow and so on) on top of what you described. It is under **Advanced** and is optional.
- Q: What is under **Advanced**?
  A: The things most hosts never need: camera move for clips, look, quality, resolution or picture size, and **Auto-improve description**. The defaults are already the ones we recommend.
- Q: Can I use an AI photo in a post?
  A: Yes. Open the ⋯ menu in the workspace and tap **Use in a post**. A new post opens with that photo as the background. **Make a clip from this** starts a clip from it instead.
- Q: How do I save or share what I made?
  A: **Download** saves the version on screen to your device. **Publish** posts it to your connected Facebook Page or Instagram.
- Q: Where do I see what I already published?
  A: In any tab, open **History** at the end of the toolbar (**More** on a phone) and tap **Recent publishes**. The same menu holds **Recently deleted**.
- Q: How is AI Studio different from the AI button in the editors?
  A: AI Studio makes the finished thing and keeps it as a conversation you can improve. The AI button inside an editor fills in one item you are already working on.
- Q: Does Generate use my AI credits?
  A: Yes. Each option shows its cost and the total is on the Generate button before you run it. Your monthly allowance depends on your plan. Shuffling a layout or changing a post's size is free.
- Q: Which plan do I need?
  A: Posts use the same plan as the other **Generate with AI** buttons. Photo needs Pro and above and Clip needs Business and above. On lower plans you can still fill in the form; Generate shows which plan unlocks it.
- Q: What happens to things I already made if I downgrade?
  A: You keep them. Your creations, downloads and publishing all keep working; you just cannot make new ones until you upgrade again.
- Q: My prompt was blocked. What now?
  A: Some subjects are refused. Rephrase and try again. You are not charged for a blocked request.
- Q: A version failed. What now?
  A: The preview says what went wrong and offers **Try again**, which runs the same request once more.
- Q: Can I generate videos?
  A: Yes on Business and above. Tap **New clip**, pick one of your photos to start from, choose a camera move under Advanced, then Generate. The description is optional when you start from a photo. Clips are 8 seconds and 1080p by default (Draft is 720p). Sound (ambient or music) is under Advanced.
- **Q: Will the video change my room?**
  A: It starts on your photo and is told to keep the space as photographed, with no new furniture or people. Small changes can still happen, so check the clip before posting.
- Q: What is Premium quality?
  A: It is off unless the platform team turns it on for your property. When it is on, it shows as a third Quality option under Advanced.
- Q: I deleted something in AI Studio by mistake. Can I get it back?
  A: Yes, for 30 days. Tap **Undo** right after deleting, or open **History**, choose **Recently deleted**, and tap **Restore**. On a phone, that menu is **More**. Deleting a single version inside a conversation is permanent, and posts you already published stay up.
- Q: What is the **Property colors** theme?
  A: A built-in theme made from your property photos. If there are no photos, it uses your brand color. Change your photos and it updates, along with items made in it. Items you made before themes existed live here.
- Q: Does **Generate with AI** follow my theme?
  A: Yes. In a theme the AI picks the layout and words, and the theme sets colors and fonts. The Look step only asks for a mood.
- Q: How do I rename something?
  A: Tap its name in the editor's top bar, or use **Rename** in the card's **⋯** menu.
- Q: Where do I set up marketing Telegram alerts?
  A: Those live on the **Notifications** page under the Marketing module, not on this Content Studio page.
- Q: How do I make a multi-photo collage (a 2x2 grid, a before/after, a photo strip)?
  A: On **Posts**, tap **Collage**. Pick a layout, then **Fill all** to drop in your gallery photos at once, or tap a cell then tap a photo to place it one at a time. Drag cells to swap their photos, and use **Adjust photo** to reposition a photo inside its cell without changing the layout. You can still add text, stickers, and your logo on top afterward.
- Q: I started a collage. Can I still add text or a logo on top?
  A: Yes. Collage cells are normal photos on the same canvas; use Elements, Text, or the Logo option exactly as you would on any other post.
- Q: What does **Blank** do?
  A: Opens an empty page in the size you picked so you can build from scratch.

## Marketing Content Studio

Property-scoped **Marketing Content Studio**: build availability calendars, social graphics, and short promo videos, then publish to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.

### Themes

A theme is the look every item in the workspace follows. URL state: `?theme=<key>&tab=<calendar|design|video|generate>`; `?view=themes` opens the theme library. Without a theme in the URL the page opens the last theme used on this property (`localStorage` `marketing-last-theme:{propertyId}`); on the first visit it opens the library.

| Kind                                                | Key                          | Stored                                                | Editable                 |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Property colors                                     | `property-colors`            | Code (derived from property photos, else brand color) | Customize → saves a copy |
| Starter looks (Quiet Coast + the 14 calendar looks) | `starter:<id>`               | Code                                                  | Customize → saves a copy |
| Saved theme                                         | `marketing_themes.id` (uuid) | `marketing_themes` row                                | Edit in place            |

**Tokens** (`marketing_themes.tokens`, validated by the same parser on client and edge): `palette {primary, secondary, accent, surface, ink}` (hex), `fonts {display, body, label}` (allow-listed families), `background` (`solid` · `soft-gradient` · `pattern` · `photo-wash`), `corners` (`sharp` · `soft` · `round`).

**Theme library (`?view=themes`):** the root of Marketing themes. The dashed **New theme** tile (**More looks** for roles without add access) is always the first card and opens the New theme dialog. After it, only the host's themes (Property colors, saved themes, starters that already hold items, plus the starter currently open). Each card (`ThemeShowcase`) always shows sample art: a post, the theme's calendar layout, and a clip, painted from the theme colors, fonts and cover photo. Saved designs are not used as the card, so a blank or half-finished canvas never replaces the sample. No capture runs. Card ⋯ menu: Edit / Customize, Duplicate, Delete (saved themes only). The open theme is marked **Open now**. The library has no back control to the open theme.

**New theme dialog (`NewThemeDialog`):** **Describe a look** (AI draft, below) and **Start from a look**: Your property colors plus one starter per group, **More looks** reveals the rest. Picking one opens the editor in create mode with that look (`source` `starter`/`photos`, `sourceRef` = the look). Without `customTemplates` (or without add access) picking a look opens it as it is instead. A theme created from a starter (or a copy of one) opens the calendar on that starter's layout (`calendarLayoutId` inherited through `sourceRef`).

**Theme bar (`ThemeContextBar`):** **Themes** › theme name ▾ · **Edit theme** / **Customize**. Desktop: right end of the studio card's tab row. Phones: the first row of the studio card, with icon-only Themes and Customize. **Themes** opens the library. The name opens a dropdown (bottom sheet on phones) with your themes and item counts, **New theme** and **All themes**. Edit / Customize opens the editor for the open theme.

**Edit theme sheet:** two columns on desktop, preview on top on phones (sticky). Preview: `ThemeShowcase` sample art (post, calendar layout, clip) that updates as colors and fonts change, plus a headline / body / buttons specimen on desktop and a readability note listing low-contrast pairs. Controls: name; **Palette** (up to 8 whole palettes from Your photos, your themes and the starters, near-duplicates dropped, in a wrapping grid); **Colors** (five swatches: Main, Highlight, Soft tone, Background, Text; tapping one shows lighter / darker shades, other colors used by the looks, and **Any color**, the device color picker; no color codes are shown); **Fonts** (pairings with live specimens, **Pick each font** for display / body / labels); **Background** and **Corners** as visual tiles; **More options** holds **Photo mood** (optional `tokens.photoLook`, one of the AI Photo looks; AI Studio's Photo / Video composer starts with it selected). **Describe a look** (AI draft: `generate-marketing-template` `contentType: 'theme'`, plan `aiMarketingGeneration`, permission `marketing.generate:add`; fills the draft only, nothing is saved; a theme saved from it gets `source: 'ai'`) lives in the New theme dialog. Unsaved changes prompt on close. Saving a built-in creates a copy (`source` `starter`/`photos`) and opens it.

**Save paths**

1. Create: `POST marketing-themes` `{ name, tokens, source, sourceRef? }` → row (max 40 per property; name 1 to 60 characters, spaces collapsed).
2. Edit: `PATCH marketing-themes` `{ id, name?, tokens? }`.
3. Delete: `DELETE marketing-themes` `{ id, moveTo? }`. When items use the theme and `moveTo` is missing the edge returns **409** with `itemCount`; the dialog always sends `moveTo` and the items' `theme_key` is rewritten before the row is deleted.

**Items and themes:** every save sends `themeKey` (`marketing_templates.theme_key`) and stamps `designJson.themeTokens` + `themeHash` (+ `lockedPaths` for calendars, `lockedElementIds` for posts). Each editor shows and autosaves only the open theme's items, and remounts when you switch themes. Rows from before themes have no key: calendars edited from a designer look belong to that look's starter theme (`starter:<presetId>`), everything else to Property colors. `GET marketing-themes` counts items per key with the same rule.

**How items follow a theme edit:**

| Editor               | New items                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Saved items                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Calendar             | Layout (designer preset) dressed in the theme by `remapThemeTree`: every color moves relative to its nearest theme color, fonts by role, radii by corner style, background style when it changes; text contrast never drops below the original design (capped at WCAG targets). A starter theme on its own layout is identical to the old preset (golden test). | Re-themed from the stored snapshot on load; paths edited in **Advanced** (`lockedPaths`) are skipped.                                                                                             |
| Posts (Polotno)      | Campaign builder takes the theme palette; the four Quiet Coast faces map to the theme's display / label / body (numerals use display). New text from the Text panel uses the theme fonts and text color.                                                                                                                                                        | Re-themed from the snapshot on load; elements changed by hand or added by the host (detected against the themed baseline) keep their colors and fonts. Posts saved before themes keep their look. |
| Videos (Remotion)    | Palette and fonts resolve from the theme at render time; each template keeps its scrims, scale, pill and CTA style.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Always render in the current theme; per-layer fonts and colors set by hand stay.                                                                                                                  |
| AI (wizard, AI Post) | The AI picks layout, copy and photos; theme palette, fonts and background are applied to the result. The wizard's Look step shows the theme instead of look templates, and the type/background pickers hide. AI Post keeps the archetype's craft and script accent and swaps in the theme palette and main faces; Shuffle keeps the type.                       | —                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

**Thumbnails:** saved items show a stored image from Storage (`property-media/{propertyId}/marketing-thumbs/`, `marketing_templates.thumbnail_path`, returned as `thumbnailUrl`; long edge 480px, pixel ratio from `savedThumbnailPixelRatio`). `marketing-templates` strips `designJson.thumbnailDataUrl` on save and uploads it; older rows move a page at a time as the Library lists them. Calendars without a stored image draw a mini SVG from their saved styles (`preview_styles` from the RPC, `CalendarMiniPreview`), so the Library never mounts live calendars. Built-in post and video template art renders once per theme (`themeHash` in the cache key, IndexedDB + in-memory LRU), only for tiles near the viewport (`IntersectionObserver`), through one global render slot that waits while the host drags on a canvas or a video plays (`marketingRenderGate`). Low-spec devices (`deviceMemory` / `hardwareConcurrency` ≤ 4 or Save-Data, `marketingDeviceTier`) show a themed outline instead (already cached art still shows) and render the real art only for the tile the host hovers or focuses. Post renders run headless in a hidden offscreen OpenPolotno workspace (export needs a mounted stage). Theme cards use `view=showcase`.

### Library → Editor

Posts, Videos and Calendar each open on a **Library**; opening an item swaps the tab content for its editor (**focus mode**: the tab row and, on phones, the theme bar hide; **Back to {tab}** returns). URL state:

| URL                                             | Opens                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `?tab=design`                                   | Library                                                                                              |
| `?tab=design&item=<uuid>`                       | Saved item                                                                                           |
| `?tab=design&item=new&from=<ref>&size=<aspect>` | Unsaved draft. `ref` = `preset:<id>` (built-in), `tpl:<uuid>` (host template), `blank`, or `collage` |

Opening from the Library pushes a history entry (location state `marketingLibraryEntry`) so browser Back and **Back to {tab}** both pop to the Library; a deep link with no entry clears the item params instead. When a draft gets its first save the URL is **replaced** with `item=<uuid>` and the editor stays mounted (no reload, no lost undo). A deleted item shows **This {item} was deleted** with **Restore**; a missing one shows **Try again** / back.

**Start row (all four tabs, same place and order).** The start buttons sit at the left of the library toolbar (where the **Your {…}** title used to be), so nothing lives in the page header: a split **New {post|video|calendar}** (the arrow opens the size menu) plus **Create with AI** (opens AI Studio on that output, or the editor's AI rail when the plan lacks AI Studio media). AI Studio shows **New post** · **New photo** · **New clip** instead, each with its plan badge.

**Library, first visit (no items in this theme):** the same toolbar row as a library that already has items: **New** and **Create with AI** on the left, **Search templates** and a **Size** menu on the right (below `lg`, the search row sits under the buttons, matching the library toolbar). The heading **Make your first {post|video|calendar}** and the template tiles follow.

**Loading.** The page title and studio card stay up. While the route chunk or the active theme is still resolving, the body is the library toolbar (start buttons, search, Filters) and an 8-card 4:5 grid. Opening a post, video, or calendar shows the editor top bar and canvas instead. Themes shows the New theme tile plus three cards (16:9 art, name, count). Recent publishes keeps its header and fills in bordered rows. AI Studio job results use a square image plus a footer button row.

**Library, returning:** **Your {posts|videos|calendars}** as a grid of uniform 4:5 cards, newest edit first, infinite scroll (24 per page). Toolbar (`MarketingLibraryToolbar`, the same component and layout on all four tabs): the start buttons (split **New {…}** + **Create with AI**) on the left — the old title + count are gone; on the right search (name), **Filters** (popover on desktop, **Refine** sheet on phones) holding size / sort (**Last edited** · **Name** · **Created**) / theme (**This theme** · **All themes**, D8), then **Select** (multi-select → bulk Delete with confirm) and a **History** menu (**Recent publishes**, **Recently deleted**). On phones those sit in the **More** sheet with **Select**. Active filters show as removable chips under the toolbar with **Clear all**. **Your templates** lists the host's own templates. Both History entries open **in place of the grid**, so neither needs its own route. **Recently deleted** keeps a **Your {posts|videos|calendars}** back link above its toolbar. **Recent publishes** puts **Back** on the same row as the title. **Recent publishes** shows for `marketing.publish:add` or content-edit permission, **Recently deleted** for `marketing.templates:delete`.

**Card:** thumbnail (stored image → calendar mini SVG drawn from saved styles → template art → wireframe), name, **Edited …** (Asia/Manila), badges **AI**, **Collage**, **N sizes** (size group). **⋯** menu (bottom sheet on phones): Open / Use template, Rename, Duplicate, Save as template, Delete. In Recently deleted: Restore, **Delete forever** (confirm), and days left.

**Template gallery (`MarketingTemplateBrowser`, also the Templates rail in Posts, Videos, and Calendar):** search, a **Size** menu (current size on an outline button, choices in a popover; a bottom sheet below `lg`), Blank / Collage starts (their preview matches the selected size), then one headed group per set: **Your templates** (rename and delete from the card menu; soft delete, 30 days), then each built-in category (Promos, Last slots, and the rest). Each group title uses the section heading, with a divider above every group after the first. Built-in templates have no rename or delete. A template used in the last 30 days shows **Used N×**; picking it asks **Continue** (opens the latest item made from it) or **Start new**. Picking a template opens a draft; nothing is written.

### Collage mode (Design)

A collage composer **inside the Posts editor's canvas**, not a separate editor and not a flattening step. Hosts start one from the Library's **Collage** tile (`from=collage`) or the **Collage** rail section in an open post; the panel (`CollagePanel`) works on the same Polotno store, so header actions, autosave, download and publish are unchanged.

- **Layout** — a grid of ~14 preset layouts (2–9 cells: side-by-side, 2x2/3x2/3x3 grids, left-hero variants, film strip, before/after, story stacks, a scattered mosaic), rendered as inline SVG thumbnails (no images, no network) with 2/3/4/5+ cell-count filter chips. Picking a layout on a blank/non-collage canvas starts a fresh collage; picking a different layout on an existing collage **relays out in place, preserving each cell's photo by position** (one Ctrl+Z undoes the whole relayout). Existing cells are reshaped rather than deleted and re-added, and the selected layout in the picker always follows the canvas.
- **Empty cells** show a flat light tint with a small "+" badge and the cell number. The placeholder image is drawn at the cell's exact size (and redrawn when the layout, gap or padding changes), so it never stretches or clips.
- **Photos** — **Fill all** drops the property gallery into every empty cell in order; tapping a cell then a photo places it one at a time. Cells reorder by dragging their sidebar thumbnails (swaps photos between the two cells, not a full array shuffle). Selecting a filled cell shows **Adjust photo** (opens Polotno's own native drag-to-reposition crop handles — no custom crop UI), **Replace** (upload a new file directly into that cell), and **Clear**.
- **Style** — gap, outer padding, corner radius, border size/colour, and background colour; sliders are debounced before they touch the canvas so dragging one doesn't thrash undo history.
- **Cells stay live.** A collage cell is a normal Polotno image element (cropped/positioned like any photo) tagged via its `custom.collageCellId` — text, stickers, and the org logo drop on top exactly as on any other design. Nothing is flattened to a single image.
- **Resize** (size menu) on a collage **resizes the canvas and relays out the existing cells** (keeps their photos); other posts use magic resize.
- **Blank** (`from=blank`) opens an empty page at the picked size. A photo handed over from AI Studio (**Use in a post**) becomes the background of a blank draft.
- **Persistence** — a collage saves through the exact same `marketing_templates` path as any other design (`content_type: 'design'`); the collage settings + layout ride inside `designJson.polotno.custom.collage`, so autosave, the saved-template gallery, thumbnails, publish, and permissions all work with zero forked code paths. Saved-template cards show a **Collage** badge (derived from `designJson.polotno.custom.collage`) so hosts can tell a saved collage apart from a saved template at a glance.
- **Plan / permissions:** no new plan key and no new permission leaf — collage inherits the existing `marketingStudio` (Pro+) feature gate and the existing `marketing.templates:add` / `:edit` leaves.

**Upload persistence fix (applies studio-wide, not just Collage):** every canvas upload — a collage cell photo, or a file dropped through Polotno's own Upload panel — now persists to Storage via `upload-marketing-asset` instead of a `URL.createObjectURL(file)` `blob:` reference. A `blob:` URL only lives for the current tab; writing one into `design_json.polotno` (the old behavior) meant the image silently vanished on the next reload. The fix: pick a file → an optimistic local preview shows immediately (no wait on the network) → the upload persists in the background → every canvas element still pointing at that preview's `blob:` URL is swapped to the real https URL → autosave is suspended for the duration so a mid-flight `blob:` URL can never be the thing that gets saved. Download/Publish additionally refuse (with a clear toast) if any element on the canvas is still a `blob:` URL, rather than silently exporting a blank frame.

### AI Studio tab (conversations)

A description plus the host's own photos in, a finished post, photo or clip out — then kept and improved by asking for changes. Unlike the other three tabs this produces the asset itself rather than a template the editors compile, so it shares none of their canvas / timeline state and none of their autosave. Work is stored as **conversations** (`marketing_ai_sessions`): one card per creation, every version inside it.

**URL state.** `?tab=generate` is the library; `?tab=generate&item=<uuid>` opens that conversation; `?tab=generate&item=new&output=post|image|video` is step 2 of a new one. **Home (library).** Nothing made yet: one centered question, **What do you want to make?**, and three cards styled like a theme card (`OutputStartCard`, mirrors `ThemeShowcase`'s tinted-backdrop-plus-frame treatment so the picker previews what each output looks like instead of just naming it) — **Post** (a sample promo design in the active theme, 4:5), **Photo** (the property's cover photo with an AI sparkle badge, square), **Clip** (a sample story frame in the active theme with a play badge, 9:16) — each captioned with its title and hint, each with its plan `TierBadge` and disabled without the matching permission ("Ask the owner for access"). Credits show on each Quality option and on the Generate button (client mirror of the server price table; parity unit-tested). Video credits (8s): Draft 400 (720p), Standard 800 (720p) / 960 (1080p), Premium 3,200.

**Video direction.** `_shared/marketingVideoPromptBuilder.ts` builds the Veo prompt in two halves. (1) A shot description: with Auto-improve on, one `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` call (`marketing_video_prompt_enhance`, platform cost, cached, 10s timeout, fail-open) **looks at the start-frame photo** and writes one shot under 110 words that keeps the picked camera move and gives motion only to things visible in the photo (curtains, plants, water, light). Off, or on failure, a deterministic sentence is built from the camera move, the host's words and property context. (2) A fixed guardrail tail appended in code, never by the LLM: real-estate videography style, platform framing for 9:16 / 16:9, "keep the space exactly as photographed, no new objects or people" (start frame) and the sound rule (no speech in either mode). The host's negative prompt is appended to a base Veo `negativePrompt` list (text, warping, morphing, flicker, cuts, plus people/faces/hands with a photo). The job stores `enhancedPrompt` / `promptEnhanced` and `generationOptions: { cameraMove, sound }`, so **Use these settings** restores the camera and sound. **Video output validation.** After download and before upload or billing, `_shared/mp4Probe.ts` reads the MP4 box headers (no decoding, no dependency). A clip under 50 KB, with no video track, more than 1.5s shorter than requested, or in the wrong orientation fails the job (`invalid_output`, host not charged; Google still bills the render, so it is recorded as platform-only cost with 0 credits and shows in the super-admin AI console). Layouts it cannot read (and fragmented files, whose header has no length) pass. The real width and height are stored on the job. Safety-filter reasons from Veo are logged server-side.

**Prompt enhancement (image only).** Before the prompt reaches the image model, `_shared/marketingImagePromptBuilder.ts` expands it: a fixed system instruction frames every result as a real photograph (correct perspective, natural lighting, no plastic/waxy skin, no distorted architecture or gibberish text/signage), the chosen aspect ratio is restated as platform intent ("Instagram feed post, square crop, centered subject" for 1:1, etc.), and — when available — property context (name, type, city, residence, guest capacity, amenity labels, and brand color as a palette hint) is folded in so a short prompt like "bright living room" still produces something specific to that unit. On top of that, one cheap `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` call (`marketing_image_prompt_enhance`) rewrites the whole thing into a full scene description (subject, setting, lighting, camera) following Gemini's own prompting guidance. This call is billed as **platform cost**, not host credits — it never appears in the credits shown on the Generate button or in `credits_consumed`. It is **fail-open**: any error, empty output, or a response slower than 8s falls back to the structured (but unenhanced) prompt, so a broken enhancement call never blocks or degrades a paid generation. The **Auto-improve description** switch under More options (default on) lets a host send their exact wording instead. The job row stores both the host's original `prompt` and, when enhancement ran, the `enhancedPrompt` actually sent to the model, plus a `promptEnhanced` flag — `enhancedPrompt` is null whenever enhancement was off, skipped, or failed open, which is also how the fallback rate is measured. **Output validation.** A response that comes back 200 but with unusable bytes is rejected **before** upload and billing — the job fails instead of storing and charging for a broken image. Two layers: header-level checks (byte-size floor, aspect-ratio sanity vs. what was requested) that run for any format, plus a pixel-level check via `_shared/pngPixelSampler.ts` — a narrow PNG decoder built from Web-standard APIs already available in Deno (no new dependency) that samples luminance across the frame to catch a blank or near-uniform image the header checks alone would miss. The pixel check only covers the 8-bit, non-interlaced PNG shape Gemini actually returns; anything outside that reports "unsupported" and is skipped rather than rejected, so an edge case in image encoding never blocks a real generation.

**Generating stage.** While the POST is pending or a job is in flight, the results pane shows `AiStudioGeneratingStage` instead of a bare spinner. Minimal, in the Airbnb / Apple Photos register: a muted surface with a slow neutral shimmer, a teal home drawn as one continuous line (floor, walls, roof, door; draws, holds, clears on a 3.2s loop), the title, one crossfading status line (property wording such as Framing the space / Planning the walkthrough), and a 2px teal progress hairline on the bottom edge (an eased estimate that never reaches 100% and never shows a percentage). No gradients, glow or sparkle icon, per `DESIGN.md`. Screen readers get one coarse announcement (title, Queued, Saving), not the rotating lines or timer. Variants: `hero` (empty results pane), `card` (optimistic gallery card shaped like a finished job card, with footer skeletons so the grid does not jump when the row lands) and `fill` (covers the media area of an in-flight job card and the AI Post tiles while they design/render). With a job row it follows the real status: video shows Queued, then rotating render lines, then Saving, plus an elapsed timer (Veo can run up to ~6 min). When a photo that was generating finishes, the stage stays until the image loads, then the image fades in from a soft blur; new videos and AI Post posters use the same reveal on mount. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the home fully drawn and still, with no shimmer.

**Save path**

1. Host picks files → `prepareUpload` (lazy image optimizer) → `POST upload-marketing-generation-reference` (multipart).
2. Server sniffs magic bytes, rejects a declared/actual type mismatch, enforces 10 MB (image) / 50 MB (video), uploads to `property-media` at `marketing-ai-refs/{propertyId}/{uuid}{ext}` and inserts a `marketing_generation_references` row.
3. Host clicks **Generate** → `POST generate-marketing-media`, gated on property scope → the matching leaf (`marketing.generate.image:add` for images, `marketing.generate.video:add` for video) + plan feature → rate limit (20/5min image, 5/5min video) → option validation → `assertOrgAndPropertyAiQuota` → `assertMarketingGenerationBudget` → insert job row.
4. **Image** runs **inline** (5-15s, same pattern as `generate-marketing-template`): the bytes land at `marketing-ai/{propertyId}/{jobId}{ext}`, the job flips to `completed`, `recordAiUsage` bills real token counts (`cost_basis = 'tokens'`), `marketing.image_generated` is written, and the completed row comes back in the same response.
5. **Video** submits to Veo's `:predictLongRunning` and returns immediately with the job at `processing` (`marketing.video_generation_started` logged) — the client then polls `get-marketing-generation-job` every 3s. That endpoint re-polls Veo itself once at least 10s has passed since the last check; when Veo reports done, it downloads the clip (the download needs Google's API key header, so it must happen server-side), uploads it, bills by duration (`cost_basis = 'duration'`), and logs `marketing.video_generated`. If the host closes the tab, the **`marketing-generation-sweeper`** cron (every minute) finishes the same sequence independently, so the render is never lost — Google keeps the file for only 2 days.
6. On failure the row is written `failed` with an error code and a **short host-facing message** (never the raw provider dump). The toast and the job card show that same line. Safety blocks are **not** charged; a video that times out after 45 minutes is also not charged.

**Budget controls** (`_shared/marketingGenerationBudget.ts`), applied after the standard AI quota gate:

| Control               | Limit                                                                                                                                                                                          | Response                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Concurrency           | 2 in flight per property, 5 per org                                                                                                                                                            | 429 `retryable: true` — a wait, not an upgrade |
| In-flight reservation | month-to-date + in-flight `estimated_credits` + this job vs allowance + wallet                                                                                                                 | 429 `upgradeHook: true`                        |
| Per-feature sub-cap   | Default 60% of the org's monthly allowance per media feature; hosts can set a positive integer cap on Settings → AI Overrides; super-admin can also set it (and Premium) on org hub AI credits | 429 `upgradeHook: true`                        |

The job table doubles as the hold ledger — anything `pending` / `processing` / `finalizing` counts as reserved — so there is no separate hold table and no compensating release on failure paths.

**Version list.** The conversation detail (`marketing-ai-sessions?id=`) returns the session with its jobs and post versions; `buildAiTurns` groups them into turns (a message and the versions it made) and numbers every version. In-flight clips poll every 3s, the home grid every 5s while anything is rendering, and both stop on a terminal status or after 8 minutes without movement. A failed version shows the short reason and **Try again** (same settings, new version). A finished clip over 8 MB shows a warning line. `marketing-generations` stays the flat per-job endpoint (keyset paged, used for deletes and the editors' photo pickers).

**Downgrade behavior.** The start cards and the form stay on screen below the photo or clip plan. **Generate** carries the plan pill and opens the upgrade modal; nothing is sent. The library and every finished conversation are never plan-gated. The read endpoints gate on `marketing:view` with **no plan-feature argument**, so a downgraded org keeps its creations, the poller, downloads and publishing.

**Activity log.** A successful generation writes **`marketing.image_generated`** (info; metadata carries model, quality tier, aspect ratio, size, reference count, credits, and `prompt_enhanced`) and deleting a generated asset writes **`marketing.generated_asset_deleted`** (destructive). Deleting a conversation writes **`marketing.ai_session_deleted`** (destructive; metadata carries the count and, for delete forever, `permanent`) and restoring one writes **`marketing.ai_session_restored`**. Saving post versions, renaming a conversation and reference upload / delete are **activity-log: N/A** — steps inside a creation the generation event already records.
